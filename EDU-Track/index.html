What you get

Photo attendance

Take a photo when you log in, or from your dashboard, to mark today's attendance.

Virtual ID card

Your photo, details and barcode on a card that flips, always on your phone.

Attendance and marks

Teachers mark every class. Students see subject-wise percentages and semester results.

Daily timetable

Every department has its own timetable, with the class happening now highlighted.
